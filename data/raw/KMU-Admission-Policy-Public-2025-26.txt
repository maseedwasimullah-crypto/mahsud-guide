A. GENERAL POLICY- IMPORTANT NOTES 
 
1. The Medical/Dental colleges of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa offer 5 -years MBBS and 4 -years BDS 
courses leading to MBBS and BDS degrees respectively. 
2. The PM&DC/Government shall formulate the merit and admission criteria for the public 
colleges which is as under,  
 
MDCAT 50% Weightage 
FSc (Pre-Medical /HSSC /Equivalent) 40% Weightage 
SSC/ Matriculation /Equivalent 10% Weightage 
 
       Provided that all those candidates shall be considered eligible on merit if the student has 
acquired ≥60% marks in HSSC /equivalent, 55% marks for MBBS or 50% marks for BDS in 
MDCAT. Furthermore, it shall be the responsibility of the candidates to settle their cases 
with Inter Board Committee of Chairmen (IBCC), Islamabad (IBCC) and should get clearance 
certificate from IBCC, Islamabad for recognition of their  qualifications as equivalent to HSSC  
(Pre-Medical). 
3.  Whenever two or more candidates are bracketed equal in the final merit list, the older in 
age candidate will be preferred over younger one. In case the tie continues, the one having 
higher marks in Matric shall be considered  for admission. If the tie still continues, then the 
candidate with higher marks in entrance test will be given preference. 
4. There is no lower or upper age limit for admission in public sector Medical/Dental colleges 
of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. 
5. The government or authority may create any category of seats in the public colleges for 
purposes of admission in accordance with applicable law. No category shall be created 
which permits a student who has not qualified the MDCAT or be admitted in violation of 
the fundamental principle of merit as mandatorily applicable under Article 37(c) of the 
Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973. 
6. The final criteria for merit and admission including all category of seat shall be publicly 
announced by or before 1 st August of each year. A copy of the criteria and categories shall 
be provided to the Authority by 1st August of each year. 
7. If any public scholarship or funded program requires seats to be allocated for its program in 
public colleges, the public authority responsible for such program shall seek and obtain the 
allocation of the required seats from the respective government or authority in respect of 
public colleges administered by them by or before 1st August of each year. 
8. The admission process shall be conducted through an online portal and in a transparent 
manner with the final merit lists and admission lists being advertised publically. 
9. Admissions to medical and dental programs shall be conducted separately and each 
student shall be required to apply separately to a medical or dental program. A student 
having not applied to a given program shall not be entitled to admission to such program. 
10. Admissions to open merit seats in Public Sector Medical Colleges of MBBS  shall be 
completed by 31 st December, of each year and admissions of Public Sector Dental Colleges 
shall be completed before 15 th February, of each year . Admissions to all special category 
seats in public medical colleges not being on open merit shall be completed by 10 th January 
of each year. Admissions to all special category seats in public dental colleges not being on 
Page 5 of 37  
open merit shall be completed by 28th February of each year. 
11. The admissions in public -sector medical and dental institutions shall be completed by 
displaying at least three consecutive merit lists by the Khyber Medical University . 
Institution-wise list of all the successful candidates shall be displayed on the website of the 
KMU. In case of failure to deposit the prescribed admission fee within three working days 
of receipt of Admission offer letter , their admissions shall stand cancelled  and such 
candidates shall have no vested right of admission in that year  on that particular seat  in 
that particular institute. 
12. The vacant seats occurring on account of non -deposit of prescribed admission fee within 
the stipulated period by selected candidates in first or second merit list shall be allocated 
to candidates who are next in merit, by displaying the third merit list. The candidates may 
be upgraded to another institution in the subsequent merit list based on merit and 
preference and the fee deposited by upgraded candidates in the previous institution shall 
be transferred to new institution without any deduction. 
13. The Final list of admitted students shall be made public by or before 28th February, 2026. 
Thereafter, upon final verification by the relevant universities with whom the college is 
affiliated the verified list shall be submitted by the university to the Authority in the 
prescribed manner through PM&DC Online portal within sixty  days of completion of the 
admissions. No public college shall admit any student beyond the total s eats allocated by 
the PM&DC. 
Provided that any students admitted on a special category seat pre-defined exclusively for a 
Federal Government Program for foreign students under bilateral agreements with other 
countries who have not taken the MDCAT exam may be admitted over and above the 
allocated seats subject to such over and above admissions not being more than five 
percent of the total allocated seats of the college.  Such students shall qualify SAT -II with 
minimum score of 550 in each subject or a foreign MCAT examination or UCAT examination 
with 50% marks in the subject as prescribed in PM&DC regulations 2025, 3(c). 
14. Any allocated seat of a college which may remain vacant after the completion of 
admissions and there is no eligible candidate available all such vacant se ats shall be 
converted to open merit seats , expect foreign self-finance seats which will be converted to 
general self-finance seats. 
15.  A candidate desirous of admission in MBBS and BDS course in a public sector medical and 
dental institution of a province or a region shall apply to the concerned Admitting 
University (KMU for KP) . The Admitting University shall process the applications strictly on 
merit by preparing merit list for the concerned public medical and dental institutions. 
16. Admission schedule for public medical and dental institutions shall be advertised by KMU in 
the leading national newspapers of wide circulation as well as through its official website 
and Facebook page. Advertisement shall clearly mention the last date of submission of 
applications, dates of display of merit lists and date of deposit of fee for open merit and 
self-finance and other quota/reserved seats. 
17. The candidates shall apply for admission online by visiting http://cas.kmu.edu.pk or 
www.kmu.edu.pk. The entire process of submission of application is online only; hence 
there is no need to submit hard copy of application to the university  (KMU). The admission 
forms received by Post, urgent mail, courier service or any other means shall not be 
entertained. 
Page 6 of 37  
18. Students applying for admissions shall give an order of preferences for all the medical / 
dental colleges in the designated space provided in the Admission Form. A candidate will 
be considered for a college listed in his/her preferences. If a college is not mentioned in 
his/her preferences, admission shall not be offered in that particular college.  
19. The KMU shall ensure display of merit lists prepared in accordance with PM&DC Medical & 
Dental Undergraduate Education (Admissions, Curriculum and Conduct) Policy and 
Regulations 2025 on its own official website. In case final merit of more than one candidate 
is equal up to five decimals, the candidate older in age shall stand higher in merit. 
20. No admission form will be accepted after the due date for submission of forms. 
21. Admission to the Medical and Dental Colleges for all Provincial seats for public sector 
medical and dental colleges shall be finalized by the KMU Admission Committee (KMU -AC) 
strictly on the basis of merit -cum-choice and in accordance with PM&DC regulations 2025. 
Any admission made in violation of merit or on the basis of donations or kick back or 
unauthorized and unlawful payments in any form shall constitute grave violation of PM&DC 
regulations 2025 entailing penal action/ criminal proceedings and withdrawal of 
recognition under the Ordinance/ Law. 
22. MDCAT result of one province shall be valid for the entire country and shall be valid for a 
period of three years. 
23. No candidate will be admitted unless he/she has paid the fee and other dues. All dues must 
be paid by the due date (within three working days of placement interview or as otherwise 
mentioned in the office order for admission), failing which admission of defaulting students 
will be cancelled and next candidate on merit will be offered admission. 
24. The Institutional Admission Committees (IAC) of medical/dental colleges shall display and 
send a list of seats vacated/unfilled due to any reason, within five (05) working days after 
the completion of the placement interviews. 
25. Filling of the vacant seats shall be on the basis of merit-cum-choice through up-gradation of 
admitted students and  by candidates from  the waiting list on merit -cum-choice basis, 
which will be the responsibility of KMU Adm ission Committee (KMU -AC). Down-gradation 
to an institute which was already refused shall not be allowed. 
26. The vacant seats occurring on account of non -deposition of prescribed admission fee 
within stipulated period by selected candidates in 1 st or 2nd merit list shall be allocated to 
candidates who are next in the merit by displaying the 3rd merit list. 
27. Fresh candidates who have not deposited fee and opted to be on waiting list for high merit 
intuitions shall be offered admission on merit cum choice basis. However , the candidates 
shall not be offered admission in the intuitions which was already refused.  
28. There must be a centralized account of admitting university, w here the students have to 
deposit their admission and tuition fee etc. within the due date notified by the university. 
29. The fee deposited in KMU account shall be transferred to the concerned college/institute 
within 30 days of the display of 3rd or final merit list. 
30. Date for the commencement of classes shall be notified by the admitting university (KMU). 
31. Litigation on any category of seats in a public sector medical and dental college, with 
respect to admission, shall be the sole responsibility of KMU, who shall be liable to pay the 
concerned advocate and assist him/her in the preparation of case. 
32. KMU-AC shall decide all cases related to admission, any complaints/grievances against the 
decisions made by KMU -AC, may be challenged in Appellate KMU -Admission Committee. 
Page 7 of 37  
Review against the decision of the Appellate KMU -AC can be filed before the chairman PAC 
within 03 working days after the final selection. The decision of the PAC shall stand final. 
33. All the candidates and students are required to read, know and abide by admission policy 
of Khyber Medical University and PM&DC Medical & Dental Undergraduate Education 
(Admissions, Curriculum and Cond uct) Policy and Regulations 2025 before submission of 
application for admission. Any changes made by PM&DC in future from time to time shall 
also be applicable to admission regulations. 
34. The PM&DC shall determine and declare the ceiling for admissions permissible in public / 
private sector medical and dental institutions. For clarification the seats for each college 
shall be allocated as per  PM&DC Regulations 2025 by the Council subject to evaluation of 
each college. 
35. Candidates sent by the nominating agencies (HEC, AJK, Reciprocal, Economic Affairs etc.) 
after the closure of admission date will be admitted , subject to prior approval of PM&DC. 
Seats for Erstwhile FATA/Baluchistan project, Pakistan Technical Assistance Program and 
illegally Indian Occupied Kashmir (IIOK) will be considered as over and above of the total 
number of sanctioned seats to medical/dental institute. 
36. The reservation of quota for various categories of students for admission to medical 
colleges was declared to be without authority in line with the decision of August Supreme 
Court of Pakistan (Attiya Inayat Case) except following categories: 
(a) Disabled persons 
(b) Students domiciled in Merged Districts 
(c) Students domiciled in under developed backward districts/Tehsils/areas  
(d) Students domiciled in Azad Jummu & Kashmir 
(e) Students domiciled in Gilgit Baltistan/ Northern areas 
(f) Afghan refugees  
 
37. No institution shall be allowed for admissions against dropped out or vacant seat. 
38. All admissions will be provisional. All certificates or any other documents submitted with 
the application form will be subject to verification by the respective colleges. Any candidate 
whose documents are found incorrect or false / forged in any way, or whose authentication 
is doubtful rendering him/her ineligible for admission under the rules, will be struck off 
from the college roll and legal action may be taken under the criminal and civil law against  
him/her. 
39. The admission fee submitted by the candidate to KMU shall be forfeited in favor of the 
University and will not be refunded to the candidate as a penalty.  
40. Any certificate issued after the closing date for admission , shall not be considered for the 
purpose of calculation of merit.

B. ADMISSION COMMITTEES 
1. THE PROVINCIAL ADMISSION COMMITTEE (PAC) 
 
A Provincial Admission Committee, comprising of the following members; 
 
• Vice Chancellor, Khyber Medical University Peshawar Chairman 
• Nominee of Secretary Health, Govt of KP  Member 
• Nominee of the President PM&DC Member 
• Dean of a Medical College to be nominated by the Vice Chancellor     Member 
• Dean of a Dental College to be nominated by the Vice Chancellor Member 
• Registrar, Khyber Medical University Peshawar Member 
• Convener KMU-Admission Committee, Peshawar Member 
• Director Admissions, KMU, Peshawar    Member/Secretary 
 
The PAC shall oversee and monitor the entire admission process in accordance with 
directions issued by the admission board  of PM&DC and ensure transparent conduct of 
admission test and admissions in its area of jurisdiction. 
Any further changes by PM&DC will be adopted mutatis mutandis.

E. BASIC ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR ADMISSION INTO MBBS AND BDS  
 
Any person seeking admission in any undergraduate medical or dental college in 
Pakistan shall be eligible having fulfilled the following mandatory criteria; 
 
• Obtained a Higher Secondary School Certificate qualificati on (HSSC) or an equivalent 12 th 
grade qualification recognized in Pakistan and obtained a minimum of sixty percent (60%) 
marks therein, or a higher percentage as may be notified by the Medical and Dental 
Council; and 
• Passed the subjects of biology and chemistry and either physics or mathematics in the 
examination of the Higher Secondary School Certificate or an equivalent 12 th grade 
qualification recognized in Pakistan: 
     Provided that the mandatory eligibility requirements shall not apply to a student 
admitted on a special program seat pre -defined exclusively for a Federal Government 
program for foreign students under bilateral agreements with other countries. Such 
students shall also not be entitled to apply for a Full License to practice in Pakistan after 
having obtained their degree and will have to apply as foreign graduate’s subject to 
applicable licensing pathways. 
• The equivalence certificate must be attached with the application form before the 
closing date. Confirmation of candidate's admission will be subject to production of 
attested photocopy of equivalence certificate issued by the  IBCC Islamabad. The 
candidate has obtained marks equivalent to unadjusted 60% of FSc total marks according 
to the formula laid down by the IBCC, Islamabad. 
• The equivalence certificates of such candidates will not be accepted if he/she has not 
studied and passed the subjects of Physics /Maths, Chemistry and Biology in his/her 9 th, 
10th, 11thand 12th Grade / O & A levels or other equivalent examination recognized by the 
IBCC. He/she must have passed English as a subject at O or A level. 
• Have passed the MDCAT examination conducted by the PM&DC (at least 55% for 
admission into MBBS and 50% for admission into BDS). 
Note:  Only elective science subject’s marks shall be used for calculation of merit of those 
candidates who have passed Higher Secondary School Certificate qualification (HSSC) in 
2021 as per Peshawar High Court, Peshawar judgment through W.P # 6015/P/2023 dated 
25.01.2024. However, if the candidate has subsequently attempted/improved marks, 
his/her revised result (total marks) shall be considered for calculation of merit.

Y. MIGRATION POLICY FOR MBBS/BDS STUDENTS OF KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA 
 
Migration from one Public / Private Sector Medical / Dental College to another in   
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa will be governed by the following rules & regulations. 
1. Migration will be recommended by the KMU -AC to the Provincial Admission 
Committee and no other forum will entertain any such request. 
2. Mutual migration is not allowed. 
3. Migration from private sector to public sector medical college is not allowed. 
4. Migration will only be allowed after passing 2 nd Professional in MBBS and 1 st 
Professional in BDS examination. 
5. In case of vacant seat in any public or private medical /dental college shall inform the 
admitting university of such vacancy within 14 day in 3rd, 4th and Final Professional 
of MBBS and 2nd, 3rd and Final Professional BDS class, 
Note: Vacant Seat for migration purpose means, any seat that becomes vacant due 
to any reason. 
6. Migration will be allowed only when a vacant seat is available in the total strength 
of the college as well as in the corresponding class as approved. 
7. Migration will be allowed only when the vacant seat is available in the 
corresponding class, overall strength of the college and category of seats in which 
the student is seeking migration. 
8.  Migration will be allowed on merit in their respective category among the applicants 
based on last examination result. 
9.  Category wise migration is allowed to the students admitted against open seats, 
general self -finance and against the reserved quota s eats (Foreign Self Finance 
seats, MDs, MDs/Baluchistan Project seats, Technical Assistance Program seats, 
Backward Area seats, Minority Seat, Disabled seats, OPF seats). Category wise 
migration means that a student will only have the right to migrate if the seat in the 
same category is lying vacant in which he/she was admitted. 
10. No college shall restrict a student’s right to leave a college and transfer to  another 
college. 
11. Students seeking migration from one college to other shall fill the proforma as per 
specimen given on KMU website along with the application for migration. 
12. All the applications along with the proforma shall be submitted to Chairman KMU-
AC along with application process fee. 
13. The students shall mention in their migration application about category of seat i.e., 
either they are admitted against Open merit, Self-finance seat or MDs reserved seat 
etc. The same will be verified from the concerned college by the Khyber Medical 
University, Peshawar. 
14. While allowing migration, the rules of PM&DC must not be violated in any way. 
15. A college to which a student has transferred to and the college from which the 
student has transferred out shall inform the authority within 15  days of such  
transfer. 
16. Where a student gets migrated from one medical or dental institution to another 
medical or dental institute at any stage of the academic session, the medical or 
Page 30 of 37 
 
dental institute, as the case may be, shall be bound to refund all dues and fee 
charged proportionate to remaining period of academic session, to be counted from 
start of session. 
17. A candidate migrating to/from KP to/from other provinces shall have to provide 
NOC from both the respective college and university. NOC to candidate relieving KP 
medical/dental colleges shall be issued by the Registrar, KMU. 
18. Any student who has been enrolled by a medical or dental institution or college 
outside Pakistan and has completed more than two years of the program may seek 
transfer and admission to a medical or dental college in Pakistan subject to 
qualifying the relevant standard of the National Equivalence Board (NEB) 
Examination for the prescribed period of each program of study pursuant to which 
the student shall be qualified to obtain admission in the immediately subsequent 
year.  
19. A student shall be required to take the NEB within twelve months of having left his 
program of study outside Pakistan in order to be eligible for admission to a medical 
or dental college in Pakistan. 
20. The migration fee for Public Sector Medical / Dental Colleges of Khyber 
Pakhtunkhwa will be Rs. 50,000/ - for students who will be seeking migration from 
institutions with in the province.  
21. The migration fee will be Rs. 100,000/- for students migrating from other provinces. 
The amount will be deposited in the KMU accounts. 
22. The migration fee for Private Sector Medical / Dental Colleges of Khyber   
Pakhtunkhwa will be Rs. 200,000/ - for students who will be seeking migration from 
institutions with in the province. 
23. The migration fee will be Rs. 250,000/- for students migrating from other provinces. 
The amount will be deposited in the KMU accounts.
