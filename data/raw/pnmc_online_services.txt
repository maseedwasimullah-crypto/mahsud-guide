For Nurses, Midwives & LHVs
- New registration: apply as a nurse, midwife, LHV, or nursing assistant in five steps.
- Renew your license online.
- Request a duplicate card.
- Local verification: confirm registration status for local employers and hospitals.
- International verification: generate credentials recognised by overseas employers.
- Track application status with CNIC and token number.

For Institutes
- Institute registration: submit an institute for PNMC recognition and track the review.
- Student pre-registration: submit incoming students for pre-registration ahead of enrolment.
- Pre-registration guideline.
- Terms of reference governing institute pre-registration.

For Students
- Browse recognised institutes: search the official list before applying anywhere.
- Diploma & degree programmes recognised by PNMC.
- Admission criteria: eligibility requirements before starting an application.
- Licensing exam overview: what the Nursing Council Licensing Examination covers.
- Guides for every stage.

For the General Public
- Search the full registry by CNIC number for nurses, LHVs, midwives, community
  midwives and FWW.
- Verify a professional: the register shows who is currently licensed to practise as a
  nurse, midwife or LHV in Pakistan.
- File a complaint about a registered professional, an institute or an application.
