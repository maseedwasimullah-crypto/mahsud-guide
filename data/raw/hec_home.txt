Regulatory note recorded by MAHSUDo-guide:
HEC is the national higher-education regulator. HEC recognition of a university/degree is
distinct from programme-level professional accreditation (for example PNMC recognition of a
nursing programme). HEC publishes degree attestation and equivalence services; students
needing an equivalence certificate (for example for foreign qualifications) should consult
HEC's official Equivalence Certificate service.
